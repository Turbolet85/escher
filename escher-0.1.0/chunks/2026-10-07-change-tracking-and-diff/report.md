  - NEW public method: `dioxus_native_dom::Snapshot::diff(&self, after: &Snapshot) -> SnapshotDiff` (`snapshot_diff.rs:60`). A pure function of two `Snapshot` values: no document access, no failure path, no log, no print. A node is ADDED when its id is in `after` and not in `self`, REMOVED when in `self` and not in `after`, CHANGED when in both and its role, name, state, bounds or parent's id differ; a node whose own reading is equal is never named because a descendant's changed. `added` and `changed` follow `after`'s pre-order, `removed` follows `self`'s. It is the snapshot's diff and has NO text form, no `Display`, no JSON, no parser, no `DioxusDocument` entry point and no `Document` trait method.
