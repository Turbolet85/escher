## Patterns detected
- **Every reading the detection needs exists and is reachable through the crate's two dependencies** (`execute.rs:133-136` already calls `harness.base()` and reads a node): the snapshot's `enabled` and `bounds` through `harness.doc.snapshot()` and `Snapshot::get`; the node hit at a point through `Harness::hit` (`inspect.rs:96-98`) or `BaseDocument::hit` (`document.rs:1564`); an anonymous box's element through `BaseDocument::nearest_non_anonymous_ancestor` (`document.rs:1638`, public); a node's parent through `get_node(..).parent` (`node.rs:91`, public); the viewport's size and scroll through `BaseDocument::viewport()`, `Viewport::logical_size` and `viewport_scroll()` (`document.rs:1980`, `:2223`; `shell.rs:120-127`). Methods are called on values, so the driver names no blitz-dom type for any of them. No engine edit and no feature line is needed.
- **Each of those readers takes `&self`** — `snapshot`, `hit`, `nearest_non_anonymous_ancestor`, `get_node`, `viewport`, `viewport_scroll`, `get_client_bounding_rect` — so a detection made of them dispatches nothing, moves no hover and marks nothing in the changed set. Hover is set by `set_hover_to` on a pointer event (`document.rs:1831`), never by a hit test.
- **A pointer event's target is the hit at its page point** — `click_at(x, y)` sends a pointer down and up at one point carried as page, client and screen coordinates alike (`input.rs:18-27`, `:103-108`); the engine sets hover from the event's PAGE coordinates (`events/driver.rs:51`), and `set_hover_to` subtracts the viewport's scroll only to remember the client position before it hit-tests the point as given (`document.rs:1835-1840`). The hit walk treats fixed-position children of the root apart by subtracting the viewport's scroll for them alone (`node.rs:1417-1425`). So the hit test and `click_at` both take page coordinates, and "another element is hit at the point the action would land" is the answer of the raw hit at that page point: positive z-index hoisted children first, paint children in reverse, negative z-index last, an element with `visibility: hidden` never hit and one with `pointer-events: none` transparent to hits (`node.rs:1304-1320`, `:1388-1480`).
- **`element_from_point` is the wrong reader once a viewport is scrolled** — it documents viewport coordinates, checks them against the viewport's size, and passes them unconverted to the page-coordinate hit (`document.rs:1575-1586`). At viewport scroll 0 the two spaces coincide; after a scroll they differ by the scroll. It is not edited here (one caller, blitz-vibey-script); the detection reads the raw hit at the page point and resolves the answer to its element itself, as `element_from_point` does at `:1592-1600`.
- **The raw hit does not stop at the viewport's edge** — a point outside the viewport can still be answered through a node's scrollable overflow (`node.rs:1356-1361`), so a click aimed outside the viewport can land today and `off-screen` has to be read explicitly, from the target's viewport-relative `bounds` against `Viewport::logical_size`.
- **The executor's click point is the centre of the layout box, which a non-atomic inline element does not have** — `centre_of` uses `absolute_position` and `final_layout().size` (`execute.rs:135-143`); `get_client_bounding_rect` says of such an element "Non-atomic inline elements have no layout box of their own" and answers the union of its line fragments (`document.rs:2250-2254`). The stand has one such id-bearing element, the `span` `timer-duration-value` (`examples/seven_guis/src/tasks/timer.rs:93`). A `covered` reading at the layout-box centre would name it covered; at the centre of its client bounding rect — the snapshot's `bounds` — the hit is its own text. For an element with a box of its own the client rect is the unrounded absolute position less the viewport's scroll (`document.rs:2256-2263`), so the page point is the client centre plus the viewport's scroll, and on an unscrolled viewport it is today's point.
- **Which stand controls read not enabled at boot: 3** — `flight-return-date` (`disabled: !is_return`, `flight_booker.rs:91`), `crud-update` and `crud-delete` (`disabled: !has_selection`, `crud.rs:113`, `:128`); the fourth `disabled:` line, `flight-book` (`flight_booker.rs:98`), is enabled at boot (derivation: `grep -n 'disabled' examples/seven_guis/src/tasks/{counter,flight_booker,timer,crud}.rs` — 4 `disabled:` attribute lines and 2 CSS selectors — read against `stand_act_ids.rs:29-38`, whose doc names "the disabled controls" among the 7 of 15 a click does not change). A selector click on each already changes nothing, because the engine's click target keys on the attribute's presence (a11y-plan §5; `stand_act_ids.rs:137-142` asserts the empty diff).
- **A disabled stand control cannot hold focus** — the four are rendered through a Dioxus `disabled:` expression, which writes a truthy attribute or removes it (a11y-plan §5, Script and framework exposure), and focusability parses the value as a bool (`packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:629`; `tests/blitz-tests/tests/focusability_updates.rs:63-76`). A `press` aimed at a focused disabled control has no case on a lean-task screen.
- **The stand has no off-screen element at boot, and its scrollers are nested** — `stand_snapshot` asserts for every id of `rendered(task)` a box of non-zero size with `x >= 0`, `y >= 0`, `x + width <= 800` and `y + height <= 600` (`stand_snapshot.rs:185-197`). The two stand elements that scroll are `#task-body` (`app.rs:196-197`, `overflow-y: auto`) and the CRUD list (`crud.rs:181-182`, `overflow-y: auto; min-height: 150px`); the document itself does not overflow the viewport. Rows created past the list's box are clipped by it; what a driver click on such a row reads is not measured here.
- **`scroll_into_view` scrolls the viewport only** — it computes the aligned offset from the target's absolute position and the viewport's size and calls `scroll_to` on the root element, which the engine resolves to the viewport (`scrolling.rs:619-651`, `:272-283`). A target clipped inside a nested scroll container is not brought into view by it. With `ScrollBehavior::Instant` the viewport's scroll is written at once: `fragment_navigation.rs:220-246` asserts the offset read back immediately after the call, for each alignment. `scroll_programmatically` dispatches no `scroll` event (a TODO at `scrolling.rs:574`).
- **The driver cannot name the scroll's two enums** — `scroll_into_view` takes blitz-dom's `ScrollBehavior` and `ScrollLogicalPosition` (`packages/blitz-dom/src/lib.rs:103`); blitz-test-harness re-exports `Harness`, `HarnessOptions`, four event builders, `Rect`, `Key`, `Modifiers` and the settle names (`lib.rs:23-27`), and dioxus-native-dom re-exports `DocumentConfig` and `NodeId` of blitz-dom's (`lib.rs:30`, `:41`). A scroll therefore reaches the driver as a harness input helper, beside `apple_keybinding` (`input.rs:227-232`), which is how the harness gave the driver the delete binding at 2026-10-07-act-by-id. Whether an instant scroll made through such a helper and then settled reads `settled` in one step is not measured: no check drives a scroll through a session.
- **An id can resolve and still not be on the screen** — `element_ids()` lists every element reachable from the root, while a snapshot node needs an accessibility node and a client bounding rect (`snapshot.rs:122-129`); an element under `display: none` or `hidden` is in the first and not the second. Today a driver click on such an id resolves and runs. `not-found`'s fixed texts already describe it: meaning "no element on the screen reads the id" (`refusal.rs:60`), remedy "take a snapshot and use an id it lists" (`refusal.rs:82`).
- **Nothing stateless can tell `stale` from `not-found`** — a deleted element's id is in no `element_ids()` pair, and a detached node reads no id (architecture §Standard Contracts → Dioxus DOM bridge), so the two cases are identical to a lookup that keeps nothing: `stand_act_refused.rs:73-78` pins the deleted row as `not-found`. `stale` needs a record of ids an earlier screen of this session read; `Session` holds none (`session.rs:27-31`). Every acting call already takes the screen's snapshot before and after its step (`execute.rs:104-107`), so the ids such a record needs are already read.
- **What a sixth verb and a reworded remedy move, by count** — `VERBS.len()` is asserted 5 at `schema.rs:287`, `:294` and through `rows.len()` at `:352`, with the name list at `:283` and the shape table at `:333`; `command.rs` asserts 26 admitted rows (`:375`), 34 malformed rows (`:512`) and 5 table rows (`:569`); `refusal.rs:246-250` pins the `off-screen` meaning and remedy by text, with the remedies pairwise distinct and no `/` in any text (test-plan §4). Each moves in the same change as the text or table it pins (derivation: `grep -n 'fn \|len()' packages/escher-driver/src/{schema,command}.rs`).
- **The clean place for the nested scroll is the engine's `scroll_into_view`** (`inputs#I3`) — it is the engine's one implementation of the CSSOM View "scroll an element into view" steps, which scroll every scrolling box that holds the element, innermost first, and then the viewport; at HEAD it computes one offset from the target's absolute position and writes it to the root element only (`scrolling.rs:620-654`). Its three call sites outside the tests all mean the spec's steps: script `Element.scrollIntoView` (`packages/blitz-vibey-script/src/dom/element.rs:1288`), Dioxus `MountedData::scroll_to` (`packages/dioxus-native-dom/src/events.rs:251`) and fragment navigation (`scrolling.rs:690`). Widening it changes what each does for a target inside a nested scrolling box — today the box does not move — and nothing for a target whose only scroller is the viewport, which is every case `fragment_navigation.rs:197-262` checks. The other places are worse: a second into-view method leaves the spec'd one wrong beside it, and a walk written in the harness puts a second copy of the alignment arithmetic (`aligned_scroll_offset`, private, `scrolling.rs:588-617`) in a test crate the driver ships on.
- **Everything the nested walk needs is already in the scroll module** — a programmatic scroll of one box is `scroll_to(node, x, y, behavior)`, clamped to that box's range and never chained to an ancestor (`scrolling.rs:551-586`); which boxes scroll is `scroll_state`'s rule — `overflow` `scroll` or `auto` on the axis, and `hidden` too for a programmatic scroll (`:479-485`); the root element's scroll is the viewport's (`:272-283`); the chain an element's position is computed along is `containing_block()` (`node.rs:1564-1568`). Both absolute-position readers subtract every ancestor's scroll offset on the way up (`node.rs:1571-1590`), so an element's client bounding rect — the snapshot's `bounds` — follows a nested scroll with no further change.
- **The engine holds one scroll animation at a time** — `scroll_animation` is a single `ScrollTo` state, and a new programmatic scroll interrupts it (`scrolling.rs:136-140`, `:512-526`). Two boxes cannot both travel smoothly, and an outer box's offset computed while an inner one is still travelling is computed from the wrong position.
- **A programmatic scroll dispatches no `scroll` event** (`scrolling.rs:574`, a TODO; `:584` passes a no-op), nested or not; a nested write does stamp the box's overlay-scrollbar activity with `Instant::now()` under blitz-dom's `scrollbars` feature (`document.rs:1793-1797`), which blitz-tests turns on through blitz-paint (`tests/blitz-tests/Cargo.toml:20`), so the document reads animating for the fade's length afterwards. `Harness::settle` reports that as `Settled { animating: true }` and neither waits on it nor advances it (design-system §Motion (Animation runtime)); whether the step still reads settled is not measured.
- **The hit walk does not clip at a scrolling box's edge** — a point outside a box is still passed to its children when it lies inside the box's scrollable overflow (`node.rs:1330-1363`: the four tests are the box, the overflow rect, the hoisted content and the scrollable overflow, and only all four failing returns `None`). A click at the centre of a row scrolled out of its list can therefore land on that row, or on whatever is painted at that point — on the CRUD screen the button row sits below the list (`crud.rs:100-140`). Predicted from the code, not measured. So "is the target in view" cannot be read from the hit; it is read from geometry: the action point against the viewport and against the scrollport of every box on the target's containing-block chain whose `overflow` is not `visible`. The driver cannot make that reading itself — it would have to name stylo's overflow values, and its two dependencies re-export none — so it is one read-only reader on `BaseDocument`, beside the scroll that shares its chain.
- **The CRUD list is the stand's reachable off-screen case** — `#crud-list` is `flex: 1; overflow-y: auto; min-height: 150px` inside `.crud-body` (`flex: 1; min-height: 0`) inside a root of `height: 100%` (`crud.rs:146-183`); a row is one line of 14px text with 6px padding and a 1px rule (`:185-190`), and `crud-create` appends a row on every click with whatever the two fields hold (`crud.rs:102-109`), so the driver can grow the list past its box with clicks alone. Each row reads its author key `crud-person-{n}` (`crud.rs:69`) and the list reads `crud-list` (`:57`), so both are snapshot nodes with bounds. Predicted: rows past the box are clipped by the list; how many clicks that takes, and that it is the list and not `#task-body` that clips, is the run's to measure.
- **A result field that says so has its shape already** — `FieldKind::Flag` exists (`schema.rs:94-95`), and `advance` is the precedent of an acting verb with a sixth field and its own `Outcome` variant (`schema.rs:185-190`, `:266`; `execute.rs:32-42`).

## Conventions to follow
- **One behaviour per integration file, a `//!` doc first, both layout modes** — `for incremental in [false, true]` (`stand_act_refused.rs:1-7`, `:85`); the fixture's condition is asserted before the behaviour (`.claude/rules/testing.md` §File and naming conventions).
- **A failure message carries a mode, a task and a row index, never an id or what a screen reads** (`stand_act_refused.rs:6-7`, `:89`; `stand_act_ids.rs:8-10`).
- **Tables stated in the test with their row count asserted** (`stand_act_refused.rs:87`; `stand_act_ids.rs:113-116`, `:164-171`; `schema.rs:287`; `command.rs:375`).
- **A refused call is read from `Session::run` itself** — `act` answers `None` for every refusal (`session_common/mod.rs:140-143`), so a check that tells one cause from another matches on `session.run(&call)` (`stand_act_refused.rs:102-108`).
- **A verb is a `VerbSpec` constant listed in `VERBS`, a `Command` variant, and one arm each in `Command::spec`, `validate` and `Session::run`** (`schema.rs:204-270`; `command.rs:103-143`, `:185-203`; `execute.rs:61-94`).
- **An input the driver needs and cannot name is a harness input helper that dispatches and pumps** (`input.rs:227-232`).
- **No `unwrap`, `expect`, `panic!`, `unreachable!` or index on what a call supplies, no clock, no print or log in the crate** (`execute.rs:125-134` is the shape: a miss is `ok_or(refusal)`).
- **Every public item documented** — `#![deny(missing_docs)]` (`lib.rs:33`).
