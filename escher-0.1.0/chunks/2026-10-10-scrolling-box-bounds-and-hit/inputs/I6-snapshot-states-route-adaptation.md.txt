## The founder's ruling

Asked on 2026-10-10, by question dialog in the overseer session, whether those states belong in 0.1.0 — three
options put to him whole: in 0.1.0 as their own chunk · measured at "Stand requirement sweep" and decided then · a
residual for 0.2.0 — the founder chose «В 0.1.0, отдельным чанком». That label is the overseer's Russian wording for
"in 0.1.0, as a separate chunk"; the ruling is his, relayed by the overseer. It widens the version's scope by his
own word.

The option as it was read to him: a new route entry in Epoch 5, after "Scrolling-box bounds and hit" and before "MCP
surface"; the engine reads `aria-selected`, `aria-pressed` (or `aria-checked` for toggles) and `aria-invalid`; a
snapshot carries the three states; the stand's tasks state them; checks in both layout modes; v010-05's wording
widens or a new requirement appears.

What was put to him as measured: the first agent trial inferred the active flight mode, the selected CRUD row and
an invalid date from side effects, in 2 of 3 tasks; the engine's accessibility builder reads only `aria-label` and
`aria-hidden` (`packages/blitz-dom/src/accessibility.rs:90`, `:97`, read by the operator); the stand marks the three
by CSS class only (`flight_booker.rs:69`, `:75`, `crud.rs:70`). Read from the code, not measured on a running
snapshot. The reasoning put to him: his own frontends are escher's first real users, and selection, toggles and
validation are in any real interface; MCP's tools and the served help then describe the full state set from the start.
