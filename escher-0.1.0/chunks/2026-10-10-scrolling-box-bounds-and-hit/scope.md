## What this chunk builds
Two fixes in the engine, each a direct change in the engine's own files, so that what the driver reads of a scrolling box is true before any later surface carries it out of the process.

- **A box that is scrolled reads its own bounds where it shows.** A snapshot node's `bounds`, for a box that is itself scrolled, is the box as it stands on the screen — the same before and after the box's content is scrolled.
- **A hit never reaches content scrolled out of its box.** A point outside a scrolling box's own area never answers a row of that box, whatever the row's layout position.
- **What follows for the driver, with no change in escher-driver's logic** — verified by reading: a target's centre is the centre of the snapshot's `bounds`, `off-screen` is read from `visible_region` and `covered` from the raw hit (`packages/escher-driver/src/execute.rs:270-278`, `:324-332`, `:336-349`). So with the two fixes: a step that scrolls a box no longer names the box among its `changed` nodes for that reason; a `click` or a `type` naming a scrolled box lands at the box's centre; a box in view is no longer refused `off-screen` at a shifted point; a control lying where scrolled-out content extends is no longer refused `covered`, and a pointer click there reaches the control. Each is measured on the fixed engine by the chunk's checks.
- **A regression check per fix, in both layout modes** (`incremental` false and true — CLAUDE.md §Critical Warnings: the two modes stay identical), on the stand and on a minimal fixture beside it where the stand lacks the case: no stand control reads the `covered` case in any state measured (`stand_act_obstructed`'s `no_stand_control_is_hit_through_by_a_row_scrolled_out_of_the_list`, green at HEAD), so that one is a fixture's. **And under both builds:** a workspace build and a per-package build compile different bodies of the bounds reader (research.md §The bounds defect), so each check is read under both.
