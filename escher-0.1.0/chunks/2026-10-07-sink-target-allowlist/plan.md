## Constraints & rejected approaches
- A second filter on the subscriber instead of the formatter — rejected: it judges a bridged record at a different place from the one that prints its target, and a filter with no level hint lifts the bridge's cheap max-level check for every `log` call at the default level (`log_tracer.rs:173`); it buys only the dispatch of records that are dropped anyway.
- Naming escher's targets one by one (`escher_telemetry`, `escher_telemetry::panic`) — rejected: three standing checks emit under `escher_driver` and `escher_stand_probe` and would have to be rewritten to keep saying the same thing.
- Admitting any third-party target, or the stand's own `seven_guis` path, to the allowlist — do not: a target is admitted only together with a scrub rule for it, and `seven_guis` logs nothing today (0 log or tracing sites in `examples/seven_guis/src`, research.md §Patterns detected).
- Starting the host with `escher_driver::start` in the new check — do not: `start` waits for the session's answer and reads no stream (`client.rs:52-62`), and the unfixed host writes about 1,068,000 B to stderr before it answers (research.md, the by-level table), so on a pipe it blocks before binding and the check would end in `start`'s timeout instead of failing red.
- A blitz-tests re-exec child that installs the sink, in place of the real binary — rejected for the host proof: it would carry `service.name=blitz-tests` and blitz-tests' feature set, not the shipped host's; the entry's subject is the session host.
- Printing captured stderr, a line of it or a needle in an assertion message, or writing the capture to a file the test leaves behind — do not: a failing run's output lands in `target/agent-run/run.log` and in the per-leg CI log uploaded on failure (obs-plan §9 → Telemetry artifact handling).
- Naming the `accessibility` feature in the seven_guis manifest to read names through the snapshot — do not (a11y-plan §1): names are read from the booted document's text.
- Editing or re-gating an engine or third-party call site, turning an engine `tracing` feature on or off, or removing the `log` bridge — do not (architecture §Conventions → Feature gating; CLAUDE.md §Critical Warnings).
- Adding a log field, a log event, an env read, a flag on `escher-session`, or a line on stdout to make the drop observable — do not.
- Claiming typed text, the upstream apps' subscribers, the WPT runner's logger, the std panic hook's raw message, or a windowed witness of the accessibility refresh — out of this chunk (scope.md §Boundaries).
