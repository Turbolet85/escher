## What this chunk builds
escher's log sink (`escher_telemetry::init`) stops printing records it has no scrub rule for. Today the formatter scrubs two things — every field of an engine-target record outside a safe list, and content-named fields at any target (`packages/escher-telemetry/src/format.rs:66-84`) — and a record from any other target prints its message and fields as written. After this chunk a record from a target outside the sink's allowlist is not printed at all.

- **The sink drops every record from a target outside its allowlist** — stated by the entry. "Drops" means no line of that record reaches the sink's writer, at every level `RUST_LOG` can name; a record that arrives over the `log` bridge is judged by the target it was logged under (its `log.target`), the way the formatter already resolves it (`format.rs:108-109`, and the unit test `bridged_log_record_is_judged_by_its_log_target`, `format.rs:220-245`).
- **The session host and the windowed stand print no id and no name at `trace`** — stated by the entry. The two binaries that install the sink are `escher-session` (`examples/seven_guis/src/session_host.rs:20`) and `seven_guis_native` (`examples/seven_guis/src/main.rs:6`) — re-read at this take-up, both lines as cited. With `RUST_LOG=trace` neither writes a stable element id or an accessible name to stderr; stdout stays empty for the host, as it is today.
- **The measured leak this closes** — verified at HEAD `ebd7411f` (research.md §Patterns detected, the by-level probe): the same scenario re-run on both builds of the host reads 0 lines at the default level, 1 line (`INFO escher_telemetry`) at `info`, 1165 lines at `debug` and 1501 at `trace` (about 1.1 MB — 1,104,892 to 1,104,899 B over three runs), every one of the 15 author-key ids of the CRUD screen on stderr at `debug` and `trace`, and the fixture rows' names and the three label texts at `trace` only, through `dioxus_core::diff::node`. - **Where the drop is applied** — `[premise-corrected: the two placements do NOT differ in re-admission — research.md §Patterns detected, read from tracing-subscriber 0.3.23 and tracing-log 0.2.0]` not stated by the entry. The scrub lives in the formatter today; a drop can live there or ahead of it, as a second filter on the subscriber. Under either a `RUST_LOG` directive naming a third-party target cannot re-admit its records: the formatter is the last thing a record meets, and stacked filters all have to enable a record. What does differ: in the formatter the target is resolved once, by the code that already prints it, and nothing is added to the subscriber; a filter spares a dropped record its dispatch but judges a bridged `log` record at a second place and carries a max-level trap (a filter with no level hint lifts the bridge's cheap level check for every `log` call at the default level). The plan states the choice.

Measured at HEAD (research.md, the by-level reading): about 856,000 B at `debug` and 1,068,000 B at `trace` reach stderr BEFORE the socket first answers, so the reading has to start at the spawn — `escher_driver::start` spawns the host and waits for its answer without reading anything, and with a piped stderr the unfixed host would block on a full pipe before it ever binds, ending in `start`'s timeout, not in a found id.
