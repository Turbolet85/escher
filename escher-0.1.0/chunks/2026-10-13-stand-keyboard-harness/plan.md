## Goal
Make the stand operable and readable by keyboard alone, and prove it headlessly. Today no key activates anything:
measured through the driver at P3, Tab focuses `counter-increment` and `press enter` / `press space` each return an
empty diff while a click counts (`.andromeda/runs/2026-10-13T22-07-35-phase/probe-counter.txt`), so SC 2.1.1 is not met
on any stand button (per a11y-plan §1, §5). The chunk lands four rulings and a harness that proves them:
three direct engine changes for every document, ratified by the founder — Enter and Space run a focused control's
click (F1), a click on a button focuses it (F2), any `disabled` attribute removes focusability (F3) — and one stand
markup change by the operator's answer — the CRUD list takes focus and its arrows move the selection (F5). The fifth
fork, `Node::absolute_position`, is deferred by the founder's word (F4) and re-homed.

The harness is one new blitz-tests target, `stand_keyboard`, booted through `seven_guis::stand` in both layout modes:
each lean task's whole Tab sequence forward and back with wrap-around, the three focus readings agreeing at every step
(the stable id, the snapshot's one `focused` node, the accessibility tree's focus `author_id`), a stated activation
table whose keyboard outcome equals the pointer outcome row for row, the click-focus reading, the disabled-attribute
skip, the list's arrow keys, and the every-OS witness of the Apple delete binding (per a11y-plan §3, §5; test-plan §1,
§3; arch §Conventions → Tests). Telemetry is untouched: no log site, field or span is added (per obs-plan §2, §3, §4).

**The known gap, stated plainly (the operator's condition, inputs#I4):** with F5 the keyboard focus sits on the list
(`crud-list`), never on a row; the engine's accessibility builder does not read `aria-activedescendant`, so the tree's
focus is the list, not the option, and an assistive technology learns the current row only from its `selected`
reading. The fix is owned by a new CARRY on "Stand a11y assertions" (escher-0.1.0/working-route.md:94), beside the
listbox-name CARRY already there (`## Beyond the plan` on the review card).
