- **One span per driver command** — a call handed to `Session::run` opens one span and closes it when the call returns; six verbs (`snapshot` · `click` · `type` · `press` · `advance` · `scroll`), one span each, none nested per verb.
- **The span covers the whole call, the check included** — two of the eight causes are returned by `validate` before anything runs (`execute.rs:83`), so a span that is to carry every refusal cause opens in front of `validate`, not after it.
- **It reads three things** — the entry's words: "settle wait, diff size and refusal cause".
  - **Settle wait** — each acting verb settles the instance once (`settled_step`, `execute.rs:257-267`: one `Harness::settle`, whose answer is `Settled` or `NotSettled` naming the busy class — `packages/blitz-test-harness/src/settle.rs:151`).   - **Diff size** — the count of nodes a step's diff names: added, removed and changed (`SnapshotDiff`, `packages/dioxus-native-dom/src/snapshot_diff.rs:13-22`). Numbers only; never a node, an id or a name out of the diff.
