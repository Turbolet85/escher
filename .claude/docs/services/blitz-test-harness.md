# blitz-test-harness

_Crate notes. Primary source: `.andromeda/test-plan.md` §3 and `.andromeda/architecture.md` §Standard Contracts Test harness._

## Responsibility
The headless harness for Blitz documents (unpublished): wraps any blitz-dom `Document` (`HtmlDocument`, `DioxusDocument`) with deterministic construction defaults, a pump/tick loop, `settle` (that pass repeated until no work is due), input synthesis through the real event-dispatch pipeline, inspection helpers and, since 2026-10-12-headless-screenshot, a screenshot — the viewport painted through blitz-paint into `VelloCpuImageRenderer` and composited over opaque white, a paint with no display — and, since 2026-10-13-stand-contrast-harness, a contrast reader, `Harness::text_contrast`, reading a control's text colour against its composited background from computed styles and changing nothing. No window, GPU or compositor. It is the measured starting point for escher's headless stand and driver.

## Key integrations

### Consumes from
- blitz-dom (built with `accessibility`), blitz-html (`HtmlProvider`), dioxus-native-dom, keyboard-types and, since 2026-10-12-headless-screenshot, for the screenshot `blitz-paint` (`default-features = false`, so no `svg` and no `scrollbars` in a per-package build), `anyrender` and `anyrender_vello_cpu` (no `multithreading`) and, since 2026-10-13-stand-contrast-harness, for the contrast reader `color` (`{ workspace = true }`, already in the graph through blitz-dom; the harness still takes no `tracing`).

### Publishes to
- `Harness`, `HarnessOptions { width, height, scale, color_scheme, base_url, net_provider, font_ctx, incremental }` (`font_ctx` → `DocumentConfig.font_ctx`, `incremental` → `DocumentConfig.incremental`; `Default` leaves both `None`), `key_event`, `mouse_pointer_event`, `pointer_event`, `touch_pointer_event`, `Rect`, `Key`, `Modifiers` and `NamedKey` (re-exports of keyboard-types 0.8's types, `pub use keyboard_types::{Key, Modifiers, NamedKey};` — `Key` and `Modifiers` the two `press_with` takes, `NamedKey`, since the 2026-10-17 upstream sync, the named-key enum a 0.8 `Key::Named` holds — so a dependent crate names a key with no dependency of its own — escher-driver does), `Busy`, `NotSettled`, `SETTLE_PASS_LIMIT`, `Settled` and, since 2026-10-12-headless-screenshot, `PixelRect`, `Raster` and `ScreenshotError` (`FontsUnavailable` · `EmptyRect`) from the private module `screenshot` and, since 2026-10-13-stand-contrast-harness, `TextContrast` and `contrast_ratio` from the private module `contrast`, both re-exported at the crate root.
- `Harness::settle() -> Result<Settled, NotSettled>` — runs every pass that is due, then says whether anything is outstanding: `Settled { passes, animating }`, or `NotSettled { busy }` with `Busy` one of `Render` · `Layout` (still busy after `SETTLE_PASS_LIMIT` = 64 passes) · `Loads` (a load in flight, reported at once). The outcome names a class only — no node, id, name, URL or value. Since 2026-10-12-headless-screenshot `Harness::screenshot(&mut self) -> Result<Raster, ScreenshotError>` and `screenshot_rect(&mut self, PixelRect) -> Result<Raster, ScreenshotError>` resolve at the harness's own time (no tick, no pump), paint the viewport at its scale and device size into `VelloCpuImageRenderer` and composite over opaque white (premultiplied source-over); a rectangle is clipped to the viewport and an empty one is `EmptyRect`; a document whose `can_paint_text` reads false is `FontsUnavailable` with nothing painted; no path prints, panics or logs. Since 2026-10-13-stand-contrast-harness `Harness::text_contrast(&self, NodeId) -> Option<TextContrast>` reads and changes nothing (no `doc.mutate()`, input, pump or settle; every node by `get_node`): the text-bearing element — the node itself when it is a text input, else the parent element of the first text node under it holding a character other than whitespace — gives its computed `color`, `font-size` and `font-weight`, over a background composited source-over from each element's `background-color` up through its ancestors to the first opaque one, else onto the canvas (the root element's background, else `body`'s) over opaque white; it reads `None` for no such text, for an element on the walk without primary styles, and for one up to the first opaque whose `opacity` is below 1, whose `filter` list is not empty or whose `background-image` holds a layer other than `none`; `TextContrast { text, background, ratio, font_size_px, font_weight }` has `is_large_text()` (at least 24 px, or 14 pt at weight 700 or more) and `required_ratio()` (3.0 for large text, else 4.5); `contrast_ratio(a, b) -> f32` is the WCAG 2.x ratio `(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)` over the `color` crate's relative luminance, alpha ignored; it opens no span and prints or logs nothing.
- Consumed by seven_guis' headless stand (`seven_guis::stand::{boot, boot_timer, boot_cells, boot_load, options}` — `boot_load` replacing its options' `net_provider` with the stand's `LoadNet` through `..options`, every other boot offline), which builds its pinned options as a full `HarnessOptions` literal, and by escher-driver, whose `screenshot` verb's frame is the harness's `screenshot` (since 2026-10-12-headless-screenshot) and whose `advance` moves the harness's clock by exactly `ms` through `Harness::advance_time` inside one settled step, then hands `ms` to the session's time step where one was handed (since 2026-10-18-one-virtual-clock, ratified by the founder, 2026-10-18), and whose `Session::lint` (since 2026-10-19-visual-lint; in process and, as the `lint` verb, the eighth instance verb, on every carrier) reads its `contrast` defect through `Harness::text_contrast`, reported below `required_ratio()`, exempt at `enabled == Some(false)`, `None` not measured, and whose acting steps (since 2026-10-20-load-wait) settle through `Harness::settle` again every 10 ms while it answers `NotSettled { busy: Busy::Loads }`, for at most `LOAD_WAIT` (5 s of wall time), moving no harness time.

## Internal conventions
- Constructors pump once; `wrap` does not. The harness's time (since 2026-10-18-one-virtual-clock, ratified by the founder, 2026-10-18) is a `VirtualClock` its documents share: `HarnessOptions::into_config(self, &VirtualClock)` puts a clone in `DocumentConfig.clock`, so `from_html_with` and `from_vdom`, and through them `from_html` and `from_component`, build documents that read it, while `wrap(doc)` gives the harness a fresh `VirtualClock` and the wrapped document keeps the clock its own config gave it; `time()` reads that clock in seconds; `tick(dt)` advances it, a negative or non-finite `dt` moving nothing, and `advance_time(&mut self, by: Duration)` advances it by exactly `by`, each then pumping; `screenshot`, `screenshot_rect`, `text_contrast` and `settle` never move it. `pump` polls with no waker and resolves at harness time; `dispatch` / `dispatch_recorded` do not pump; input helpers pump after dispatch — `apple_keybinding(command)` among them, which dispatches `UiEvent::AppleStandardKeybinding` to the focused element as a macOS window does for a standard key binding (`deleteBackward:`); exercised on every OS since 2026-10-13-stand-keyboard-harness by `stand_keyboard::the_apple_delete_binding_deletes_on_every_os` (green on the linux, windows and macOS legs), and on the macOS CI leg by the driver's `press backspace` in `stand_act_keys` and, since 2026-10-10-driver-cli, by `stand_act_filled`'s clearing test (a `type` with an empty text deleting through the driver's own Backspace press) — and `scroll_into_view(node_id)`, which calls `BaseDocument::scroll_into_view` at once and by the least movement on each axis (every scrolling box that holds the node, then the viewport); it has no check of its own: the driver's `scroll` calls it, and `stand_actionable_keys` scrolls each Home card into view with it before a raw click. A raw `click` on an element lying outside the padding box of a box that clips it does not land on it; and for a box that is itself scrolled `layout_rect`, `layout_rect_of` and `center_of` read where the box stands — a box at (40, 50) reads (40, 50) at every scroll offset of its own, in both layout modes — as `BaseDocument::get_client_bounding_rect` and the snapshot's `bounds` do (built on `Node::absolute_position`; `scrolled_box_absolute_position` its check, ratified by the founder, 2026-10-18).
- One settle pass is `pump`'s two calls with poll's answer kept; a pass is quiet when poll answered `false`, the hover node did not change and no load finished in it. Settle reads no clock but the harness's, advances no time (a timer or an animation is the caller's to move; a running animation is reported as `animating`), never waits on a load, neither reads nor drains the changed set, logs nothing. No input helper settles — they still pump once.
- Loads are counted on the `net_provider` the harness was constructed with: a provider that fetches is wrapped in a crate-private counter (counts only); an offline harness (every stand boot but `boot_load`'s) and a document passed to `wrap` carry none; the stand's load page, whose `LoadNet` overrides no `is_noop`, carries the counter, so its one stylesheet fetch reads as a load in flight until it is answered and are read through `has_pending_critical_resources()` alone.
- `dispatch_recorded` installs a `RecordingHandler` on the underlying `BaseDocument` — it bypasses Dioxus VirtualDom forwarding.
- `dom_string()` is a stable one-node-per-line tree with geometry.

## Crate-specific gotchas
- The `query` helper panics with "invalid selector" on a selector that fails to parse.
- Synthesized pointer events set page = screen = client coordinates.

## Entry points for modification
- `src/harness.rs` (construction, pump, dispatch) · `src/settle.rs` (the settle loop, its outcome types, the load counter) · `src/input.rs` (input synthesis) · `src/inspect.rs` (queries, `dom_string`) · `src/screenshot.rs` (the screenshot: `Harness::screenshot`, `screenshot_rect`, `PixelRect`, `Raster`, `ScreenshotError`, since 2026-10-12-headless-screenshot) · `src/contrast.rs` (the contrast reader: `Harness::text_contrast`, `TextContrast`, `contrast_ratio`, since 2026-10-13-stand-contrast-harness)

## Testing this crate
- `cargo test -p blitz-tests --test harness_smoke` — end-to-end smoke for HTML and Dioxus documents.
- `cargo test -p blitz-test-harness --lib settle` — the crate's 11 settle unit tests (the load counter, the loop over a scripted document, the messages); `cargo test -p blitz-test-harness --lib screenshot` — its 6 screenshot unit tests (22 in the crate since 2026-10-13-stand-contrast-harness, 17 before it; the six: the whole viewport at its device size, unpainted reading opaque white, a rectangle clipped or an error, a document with no font refused, a shot the same twice); `cargo test -p blitz-tests --test stand_settle` — settle on the stand and on fixtures, both layout modes; `cargo test -p blitz-test-harness --lib contrast` — its 5 contrast unit tests (since 2026-10-13-stand-contrast-harness: the WCAG 2.x ratio table, the large-text threshold table, a button reading its own text and background, a translucent ancestor composited over the page, an element with no text reading `None`); `cargo test -p blitz-tests --test stand_contrast` — the 17 `controls(task)` rows of the four lean tasks at boot read through `text_contrast` against a stated record, and the painted background matching the measured one, both layout modes; 5 tests (2 until 2026-10-14-stand-a11y-assertions); since that chunk it also asserts SC 1.4.3 on every non-exempt control row, the CRUD list rows and the text outside the controls (`TEXT_ROWS`, 14, all pass) and the colour states it reaches, hover and active included (`STATE_ROWS`, 18: 17 pass, 1 exempt), and runs in the `a11y` CI leg; since 2026-10-14-quality-gates the `stand-repeat` leg runs `stand_settle` and `stand_contrast` three rounds with a zero-failure budget, and the `coverage` leg holds this crate to a 90 % line floor (`packages/blitz-test-harness=90` through `coverage_floor.py`; read 91.69 % at that chunk); since 2026-10-18-one-virtual-clock the harness's time is read by `cargo test -p blitz-tests --test virtual_clock_engine` (5 tests — 4 until 2026-10-19-session-recording — both layout modes: the scrollbar fade moving only with the clock, two clicks a double click by the clock alone — its document driven through `Harness::wrap` — a smooth scroll standing still until the clock moves, a finger pan and its fling moving only with the clock, and a document built with no clock fading by the wall clock) and `cargo test -p blitz-tests --test stand_virtual_clock` (2 tests through `Session::run`, both layout modes, reading `harness().time()`) (ratified by the founder, 2026-10-18).
