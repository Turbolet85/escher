## Engine behaviour (measured)
- Roles: `role` attribute → HTML-AAM mapping → `Role::Unknown`; landmarks, lists, tables, form inputs by `type`, links only with `href`; a `multiple` select's `ListBox` writes `set_multiselectable`, and an `optgroup` reads `Role::Group`, its non-empty `label` attribute its name, named in the tree and unpainted — what an assistive technology reads of them is not measured.
- Hidden: `hidden` / `display:none` / `visibility:hidden` exclude the subtree — an `option` of a `select`, its child or under its `optgroup` (`is_select_option`), excepted from `display: none`, which every option of a single-choice select but its chosen one computes and a `multiple` select's rows do not: it stays in the tree, `hidden`, `inert` and `visibility: hidden` still excluding it; `aria-hidden` marks hidden; an element carrying `inert` is excluded with its subtree too, and so from the snapshot, and a hit passes through it, removing `inert` restoring the tree and the hit (the `inert` read ratified by the founder, 2026-10-18).
