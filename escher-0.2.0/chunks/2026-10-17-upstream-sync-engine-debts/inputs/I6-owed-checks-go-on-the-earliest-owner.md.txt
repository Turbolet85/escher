When a chunk leaves an edited code path with no check, the route owner for that owed check is the earliest markerless entry that plausibly takes it — not the catch-all "Quality gates" entry near the route's end.

**What happened (2026-10-10, the wrap of 2026-10-10-scrolling-box-bounds-and-hit):** I proposed "Quality gates" for two unchecked paths (a box both transformed and scrolled; a scrolled box under a vertical writing mode). The operator moved both to "Stand requirement sweep", in their words: "they are checks owed on code this chunk changed, and the sweep comes first."

**Why:** a check owed on code that already shipped should be written as soon as an entry can take it; an entry late in the route leaves the path unchecked for longer.

**How to apply:** when proposing owners at a wrap's P5, order the candidate entries by position and lean to the first one whose scope fits, saying why. My interpretation, not the operator's words: this is about owed checks on shipped code, not about every open finding — a decision question ("does the fork gate on licences") can still belong on the entry that decides that class.
