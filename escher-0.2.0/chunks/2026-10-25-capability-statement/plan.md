## Implementation notes
### Statement content (each item required; the source after it)
**What it can do**
- Driver: one held headless instance per `Session`, every call through `Session::run` after `validate`. It is carried
  four ways: in process, over a unix-domain socket (`start` · `attach` · `stop` · `call`, wire `v3`, idle expiry
  1,800 s), by the `escher-session` command line, and by the `escher-session mcp` stdio role. A kept check is a fifth
  medium, at rest (arch §Standard Contracts → Driver session).
- The twelve verbs in three levels, each named: `snapshot` · `click` · `type` · `press` · `advance` · `scroll` ·
  `screenshot` · `lint`, then `start` · `status` · `stop`, then `help` (schema.rs:490-492).
- The eight refusal causes, each named, in their fixed order; the fifteen session errors; four exit statuses; seven
  argument kinds with bounds (refusal.rs:35; error.rs:80; schema.rs:506; security-plan §Input Validation).
- Stable element ids: four tiers, computed on demand, persistent across a re-render, a remount and a fresh process,
  carried as AccessKit `author_id`, with no `NodeId` crossing. The actionable-key check runs in process only, with no
  wire form (arch §Standard Contracts → Dioxus DOM bridge).
- The snapshot: id · role · name · state · bounds, thirteen node keys; its text form under the 10,000-byte budget; its
  parts (`snapshot --part`, 1 to 1,000); its diff by stable id, with `differs` naming the readings that moved (arch
  §Standard Contracts → Dioxus DOM bridge).
- Acting and settling: each acting verb is one settled step returning the diff; the refusal order `stale` /
  `not-found`, then `disabled` · `off-screen` · `covered`; `type` replaces what a control holds, and on a select it
  chooses by label prefix; `scroll` goes through every scrolling box and answers `in_view`; the load wait is `LOAD_WAIT`,
  5 s of wall time, on the five acting verbs, moving no time (arch §Standard Contracts → Driver session).
- One virtual clock: `advance` moves the session's clock by exactly `ms`; two `click`s on one point with no `advance` of
  500 ms between them read as a double click — (sampler) a11y-plan/keyboard-navigation#2.4.
- The headless screenshot: a PNG equal to the harness raster, the whole screen or one element's visible part,
  `fonts-unavailable` when no `sans-serif` family resolves (design-system §Typography).
- The visual lint: six defects with ids, closed words and numbers only, changing nothing (arch §Standard Contracts →
