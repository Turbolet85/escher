## 5. Keyboard Navigation

<!-- detail: values · keyboard-focus-order -->

- An element is focusable if it holds a sub-document, or if it is not inert (by presence on itself or an ancestor element, `Node::is_inert`), is no disabled form control and either has `tabindex >= 0` or, with no tabindex, is an `<a>`/`<area>` with `href` or a `button`, `input`, `select`, `textarea`, `frame`, `iframe` or `summary` (packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:742-774) — `disabled` read by its presence on a form control alone (`ElementData::is_disabled`: `can_be_disabled()` and the attribute present), any value, bare, `"false"` or `"true"`, so a `<div disabled tabindex=0>` takes focus; the Tab walk (`focus_next_node` / `focus_prev_node`), autofocus (a disabled form control or an inert candidate skipped) and `DioxusDocument::unkeyed_actionable` read it (packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:516-520; packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:743; packages/blitz-dom/src/node/node.rs:574-591; tests/blitz-tests/tests/stand_keyboard.rs:889-930) — the presence reading ratified by the founder (2026-10-13, «По наличию (Рекомендую)», confirmed on the wrap's answer card on 2026-10-14 «Подтвердить (Рекомендую)», each by question dialog in the overseer session, relayed verbatim by the overseer as his chosen option label), its narrowing to form controls and the `inert` read ratified by the founder (2026-10-18); HTML's focus fixup is built, in every document, so a node that holds focus when it becomes inert gives focus up at the flush, no DOM `blur` or `focusout` firing (the line below)
- `DocumentMutator::flush` calls `BaseDocument::clear_focus` where `focus_node_id` resolves to a node reading `is_inert()`, in every document, with no `inert` arm joining the attribute writes, so a subtree moved under an inert box is covered too; no DOM `blur` or `focusout` fires, and `disabled` and `hidden` take no part in it (packages/blitz-dom/src/mutator.rs:1123-1135; tests/blitz-tests/tests/inert_subtree.rs:229-310) — as measured at escher-0.3.0/chunks/2026-10-31-engine-debts/report.md
- The limit that stays: the fixup reads `is_inert()` alone, so a focused control that no longer shows keeps focus and takes keys, a select's choice still moving past its dropped anonymous block, ratified (tests/blitz-tests/tests/key_press_after_container_lost_its_box.rs:136-160) — as measured at escher-0.3.0/chunks/2026-11-01-upstream-sync-ui-graph/report.md
- That limit is owned by a line in `.andromeda/residuals.md`, a line in `escher-0.4.0-incubator/deferred-from-0.3.0.md` and a `CARRY:` on the entry «A11y re-read» (`chk-ajxm4sin`), its SC 2.4.3 re-assertion on the stand and served pages — as measured at escher-0.3.0/chunks/2026-11-01-upstream-sync-ui-graph/report.md
- Tab moves focus to the next node and Shift+Tab to the previous, dispatching focus events (packages/blitz-dom/src/events/keyboard.rs:30-48); `focus_next_node` / `focus_prev_node` move focus to the next/previous focussable node (packages/blitz-dom/src/document.rs:2222-2235)
- With a list open, Tab runs `choose_current_and_close` before moving focus, in either direction (packages/blitz-dom/src/events/keyboard.rs:32-34; tests/blitz-tests/tests/select_popup.rs:329-361) — as measured at escher-0.3.0/chunks/2026-10-31-select-popup/report.md
- Focus stays on the select while its list is open, and the Tab walk holds with the list open and after each close, pinned by `the_flight_type_walk_holds_with_its_list_open_and_after_each_close` and `a_click_opens_the_list_focus_stays_on_the_select_and_the_walk_goes_on_from_it` (tests/blitz-tests/tests/stand_keyboard.rs:1082-1111; tests/blitz-tests/tests/stand_keyboard.rs:1113-1134) — as measured at escher-0.3.0/chunks/2026-10-31-select-popup/report.md
- Cached focusability is recomputed when `tabindex`, `href` or `disabled` is set or removed (packages/blitz-dom/src/mutator.rs:334-343; packages/blitz-dom/src/mutator.rs:489-496); tests assert focusability follows `tabindex` set or cleared after creation (roving tabindex) (tests/blitz-tests/tests/focusability_updates.rs:1-6; tests/blitz-tests/tests/focusability_updates.rs:34-57)
- `button`, `input`, `select` and `textarea` can be disabled, which toggles the `DISABLED`/`ENABLED` states (packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:479-486; packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:512-514; packages/blitz-dom/src/node/node.rs:839-861); setting `disabled` removes a button's focusability (tests/blitz-tests/tests/focusability_updates.rs:63-76)
- Disabled-ness is keyed one way since 2026-10-13-stand-keyboard-harness: the `DISABLED`/`ENABLED` element state (hence `:disabled`) reads the attribute's PRESENCE, and the pointer-move gate, `ClickTarget::Disabled`, `handle_click`'s walk gate, the Enter and Space activation and focusability read `ElementData::is_disabled` — that presence on a `button`, `input`, `select` or `textarea` alone (ratified by the founder, 2026-10-18) — so `disabled="false"` and a bare `disabled=""` on a form control match `:disabled` and are not focusable, and a bare `disabled` set after creation takes its focusability away (packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:480-485; packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:516-520; packages/blitz-dom/src/node/element.rs:743; packages/blitz-dom/src/events/pointer.rs:333; packages/blitz-dom/src/events/pointer.rs:472; packages/blitz-dom/src/events/pointer.rs:832; packages/blitz-dom/src/events/keyboard.rs:332; packages/blitz-dom/src/events/keyboard.rs:349; tests/blitz-tests/tests/focusability_updates.rs:59-62; tests/blitz-tests/tests/focusability_updates.rs:78-96; tests/blitz-tests/tests/stand_keyboard.rs:889-930) — as measured at escher-0.1.0/chunks/2026-10-13-stand-keyboard-harness/report.md; before it focusability parsed the value as a bool and both stayed focusable (as measured at escher-0.1.0/chunks/2026-10-06-headless-stand/evidence/disabled-false-probe.txt)
- A disabled row of an open list changes nothing and leaves the list open; a disabled, inert or hidden select, or one with no row to show, opens nothing; a select made inert, disabled, hidden or removed while open closes its list (packages/blitz-dom/src/document.rs:1419-1421; tests/blitz-tests/tests/select_popup.rs:377-388; tests/blitz-tests/tests/select_popup.rs:443-472; tests/blitz-tests/tests/select_popup.rs:478-516) — as measured at escher-0.3.0/chunks/2026-10-31-select-popup/report.md
- The file input's generated inner button gets `tabindex="-1"` (packages/blitz-dom/src/mutator.rs:1558-1572)
- Under the `autofocus` feature, the latest mounted focussable node whose `autofocus` attribute is present with any value but "false" — bare, empty or "true" — is focused on flush, and `autofocus="false"` is ignored (packages/blitz-dom/src/mutator.rs:1215-1227; packages/blitz-dom/src/mutator.rs:1117-1122; tests/blitz-tests/tests/autofocus_attribute.rs:27-50) — as measured at escher-0.1.0/chunks/2026-10-10-upstream-sync-agent-surfaces/report.md
- Focusing sets the `FOCUS` and `FOCUSRING` element states; blurring removes them (packages/blitz-dom/src/document.rs:2281-2286; packages/blitz-dom/src/node/node.rs:775-813)
- `:focus` matches the focus element state and `:focus-within` the focused element and its ancestors by the `FOCUS_WITHIN` element state, while `:focus-visible` never matches (packages/blitz-dom/src/stylo.rs:491-495)
- focus and blur do not bubble; focusin and focusout bubble (packages/blitz-traits/src/events.rs:451-454)
