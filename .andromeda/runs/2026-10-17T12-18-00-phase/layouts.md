## Patterns to follow
- Record a stand change by what it leaves alone: "adding no element, class, style, wrapper or order". Cite each measured reading to its source chunk's evidence ("as measured at {chunk}/evidence/…"), as layout-templates §Surface: desktop-native / Primary screens does for every stand change since 2026-10-10.
- Record a figure by re-measuring it on the headless stand in both layout modes, then cite the chunk that measured it, as layout-templates §Surface: desktop-native / IA notes does for the `crud-list` and `task-body` readings. A moved number replaces the old one with its new source.
- Upstream additions under `packages/` and `apps/` are not stand markup. The plan's stand facts change only where a stand reading moves, the way the earlier syncs left layout-templates §Surface: desktop-native / Primary screens untouched.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Editing stand markup, CSS, ids or order to bring a moved layout number back to its recorded value. layout-templates §Surface: desktop-native / Primary screens records every stand change as adding none of these, and the scope asks for a moved number to be re-measured and recorded.
- Loosening an asserted stand figure (bounds, the `crud-list` box, Home's Cells offset) to pass instead of recording its new measured value (per layout-templates §Surface: desktop-native / IA notes).
- Adopting upstream's new ARIA reads (`aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby`, `aria-expanded`, …) in stand markup. layout-templates §Surface: desktop-native / Primary screens names the stand's inputs by `for` and `aria-label` alone, and the scope puts adopting new upstream APIs out of scope.
