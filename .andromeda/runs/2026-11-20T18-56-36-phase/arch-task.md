Settles three driver debts of 0.1.0-0.3.0 (capability v040-05), each put to the founder as a fork in one file, then fixed and proven on the stand through the driver's carriers or kept as a residual by his word.
Debt 1: the press verb holds no Ctrl, so a non-adjacent multiple select set cannot be built through the driver; the fork is whether to add a Ctrl (and by kin Meta/Alt) modifier beside the shift flag, and on which carriers, or keep the limit its help states.
Debt 2: the graph verb answers the whole UI graph at once (293,869 bytes on Cells at boot); its ruled form is graph --part N, paged as snapshot is, every part within the answer bound.
Debt 3: handler coverage reads an ancestor's handler as exercised when a bubbling dispatch stopped short of it; the fork is to read the stop in the reached record or keep the stated limit.
Re-measures the graph answer's size per part against the answer bound, and re-asserts the capability statement against the driver schema.
