# Task — design-system · 2026-10-25-cold-agent-bar

## The session
The 7GUIs flight booker's two `aria-pressed` mode buttons (`flight-one-way`, `flight-return`) and their CSS (`.flight-type-row`, `.type-btn`, `.type-btn-active`) are retired. In their place is one native select, `select#flight-type`, named "Flight type", with the options `flight-type-one-way` and `flight-type-return`.
The select's CSS: 15px, `#1a1a1a` on `#ffffff`, `1px solid #c0c0c0` border, radius 6, padding 8/10. On `:focus` it drops the outline and shows a `#3a5ce5` border, as the date inputs do. `stand_contrast` measures it at 17.40.
The stand boots two pages beside the four lean tasks: Cells (`boot_cells`) and a new load page (`boot_load`, titled "Load"). The load page holds the button `load-fetch`, in the stand accent `#3a5ce5` / white with hover `#2a4cd3` and active `#1f3fc1`, and the paragraph `load-done`, which stays `display: none` until its in-process stylesheet arrives after 200 ms. `escher-session` now serves six tasks.
Measured: at boot the lint reads one `overlap` on the flight booker (its two options sit at the select's client rect). Also measured: right after a `scroll`, the overlay scrollbar takes a click in its band as a thumb drag and stays shown until an `advance` passes its fade.
The cold-agent pipe gains six apps, new verdict fields and a 5 % wrong-call bar; a live run passed with 0 wrong calls out of 66. No engine crate, dependency or driver schema moved.
