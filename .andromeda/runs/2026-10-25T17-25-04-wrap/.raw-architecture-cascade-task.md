# Task — architecture · 2026-10-25-cold-agent-bar

## The session
The stand grew beside the four lean tasks: `seven_guis::stand::boot_cells` mounts Cells in TaskShell and `boot_load` boots a new load page whose one load is answered in process by a hand-written `LoadNet` after 200 ms; every other boot stays offline.
`escher-session` now serves six tasks (`counter` · `flight-booker` · `timer` · `crud` · `cells` · `load`) through a private `Served` table; its usage line and the MCP `start` tool's enum follow.
The flight booker's two `aria-pressed` mode buttons (`flight-one-way`, `flight-return`) and their CSS were retired for one `select#flight-type` (ComboBox "Flight type", two options); the stand now states no `aria-pressed` anywhere, and the stand's control, contrast, keyboard, act-ids and lint pins were re-measured.
The cold-agent pipe (`scripts/cold-agent.sh`) drives six apps, records its live child under `--record`, decodes paged answers and gains seven verdict fields and three reasons, `wrong_calls` deciding by the founder's 5 % bar; the founder's one live run passed (6 tasks, 66 calls, 0 wrong).
Measured: the driver lint reads a select's two options as overlapping; a served Cells screen exceeds the snapshot text budget (5 parts); right after a scroll the overlay scrollbar swallows a click in its band. No engine crate, driver schema, dependency or CI step moved.
