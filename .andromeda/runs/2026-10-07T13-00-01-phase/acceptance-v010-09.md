On the four lean stand tasks, every driver action that names an element (`click`, `type`) names it by its stable id alone, through `Session::run`, with no coordinate and no selector in the call, and takes effect on that element: for each control with an effect at boot the returned diff equals the diff a selector click yields on a twin instance and is not empty (`stand_act_ids`). `press` acts on the element an id-addressed action focused (`stand_act_keys`), and `advance` names no element.
