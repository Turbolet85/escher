# Task — obs · 2026-10-31-select-popup

## The chunk
A closed single `select` opens a painted list of its options in every document and renderer, a new engine overlay in paint, hit testing and keys; a person or agent chooses by pointer and key through the one write path `apply_select_choice`, acts on an option through the driver, or closes the list without a choice.
An open list's options read real boxes, so `snapshot`, the lint's `overlaps` exclusion, the closed-option `covered` refusal and the schema text are restated; a group's label and `size` are this chunk's to paint or read.
The focus and key contract, reduced motion and the engine's colours are the founder's P4 forks; the chunk adds no verb, node key, cause, session error, exit status, carrier, listener, port, env var or workspace crate.
`.gitignore` gains `escher-0.4.0-incubator/`, and the chunk re-reads the free-disk step in `Stand checks repeated` as its watch (0/3 green toward retirement).
