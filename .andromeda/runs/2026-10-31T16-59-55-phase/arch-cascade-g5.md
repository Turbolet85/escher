## Bindings
- **The changed set ↔ cross-cutting invalidation**: an open or close that must refresh the platform tree's `expanded` must be a write at one of the changed set's four kinds of site, or the shell never rebuilds the tree, (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:9) and (.andromeda/architecture.md:275).
- **The one write path ↔ the established select model**: the crate-private writers and `apply_select_choice` hold what the select model rules, a single select's choice of its chosen option firing nothing, (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:84) and (.andromeda/architecture.md:62).
- **Keys and focus ↔ established DOM semantics and event processing**: the queued Click and the focus event order sit under `inert`'s and the disabled reading's rules, and handlers run before the default action unless cancelled, (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:39), (.andromeda/architecture.md:70), (.andromeda/architecture.md:72) and (.andromeda/architecture.md:273).
- **Redraw ↔ service wiring**: the provider's `request_redraw` is the call that meets the pattern of a redraw after a change, skipped while critical resources are pending or the window is occluded, (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:112) and (.andromeda/architecture.md:271).
- **The harness's readers ↔ the stand's pins**: a row's `text_contrast` or `screenshot` value reads the sheet's default colours at the stand's viewport, scheme and family, so a change to any one moves the row, (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:119), (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:8), (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:135) and (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:142).
- **The document's clock ↔ the harness's time**: a reduced-motion or typeahead reading the list makes in the engine is deterministic under the harness only through the clock its documents share, (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:16) and (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:129).
- **The harness's paint ↔ renderer backends**: `screenshot` paints the overlay through the same `paint_scene` the windowed renderers use, on the CPU renderer the backends' decision names, (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:118), (.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md:105) and (.andromeda/architecture.md:40).
