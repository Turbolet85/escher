A closed select opens a painted list of its options; a person or an agent chooses one by pointer and by key and the select then reads that choice; an option of the opened list can be acted on through the driver; the list closes without a choice. Concretely, in both layout modes through the real dispatch: `select_popup` opens by click, Enter and Space, chooses by click, Enter, Space and Tab, one `input` then one `change` per change, and closes by Escape, a second click and a click outside with no event; `select_popup_painted` reads the rows on frames; `stand_act_popup` accepts `click flight-type`, then `click flight-type-return`, the next snapshot reading value `return`, and closes by `press escape` with the value unchanged.
