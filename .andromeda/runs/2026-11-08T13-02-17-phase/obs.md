@extract_section name=constraints
- per obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage (`command` span, fct-zuu6oxcq): any call reaching `Session::run` sits in the one hand-built INFO `command` span at target `escher_driver`, and that span keeps exactly its eight fields. A new read verb, if one is added, must record `verb` alone when accepted (no step run, as `snapshot`, `lint` and `graph` do) and `verb` + `cause` when refused. `verb` must be the verb table's own word, never the caller's text.
- per obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage (`tool_call` span): an MCP call of the new read must open one `tool_call` span holding only `tool` (the verb table's word) and `ending` (`accepted` · `refused` · `error`), with its `command` span as the child. No field may be added to either span.
- per obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage (never-fielded list): the read's answer must never be put in a span field. That covers its nodes, ids, owners, verb lists, per-verb refusal causes, its count or size, and any `Outcome`/`Command`/`Call`/`ArgValue`/`Refusal` (field or `Debug`). This matches the scope's span rule (fct-c7odudty).
- per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract (Logging stack, fct-icom2cq7): escher-driver and every module the chunk touches or adds (schema, execute, json, describe, mcp, recording, replay) must install no subscriber, read no env var and print nothing. The new outcome must be written only by `Outcome::to_json`, as a command's answer, as the MCP JSON line, or kept whole in a recorded check. It must never be logged, fielded or printed with `Debug`.
- per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage (escher-driver emitter): the driver must add no event. The only lines in a sink-installing process stay the closed `command` and `tool_call` spans at `info` or below, with nothing at the default `warn`. escher-driver keeps its 2 `info_span!` sites.
- per obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing & Compliance (fct-lhkfbl2j, the graph answer): the new read's answer may carry stable ids, verb names and closed cause words, like the `graph` answer that was ratified as a boundary widening. It must never carry a control's value or page text. Putting it on a carrier is a boundary widening, which is the founder's fork per the scope.
- per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract (Logging stack): checks added for the read (the stand in both layout modes and the minimal fixtures for covered, off-screen and owner-served) must install no subscriber, read no env var and print nothing. Their readings go into assertions only, and the silence probe must read 0 over them and over the shared modules they read.
