On the stand, in both layout modes, through `Session::run`: a `click` naming an id no screen of the session read is refused `not-found`; a `click` naming the id of a CRUD row the driver deleted is refused `stale`; a `click` on `crud-delete` and a `type` into `flight-return-date` while they read not enabled are refused `disabled`; a `click` on a CRUD row the driver created past the list's box is refused `off-screen`; and on a minimal fixture beside the stand, booted with the stand's options, a `click` on a target under a positioned overlay is refused `covered`. Each refusal names its cause of the closed set and leaves the snapshot of the held instance equal to the one taken before the call (`stand_act_refused` · `stand_act_disabled` · `stand_act_obstructed` · `stand_act_scroll`).
