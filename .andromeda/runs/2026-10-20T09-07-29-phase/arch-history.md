## Relevant amendment history
- **2026-10-06-snapshot-model** — registered `DioxusDocument::snapshot` → `Snapshot { roots }` with `nodes()` (pre-order) and `get(&str)`, every element the accessibility tree keeps a node, no `NodeId` in any field, under `accessibility`. This is the tree a part-cut splits, and "every node exactly once" is counted over `nodes()`. Its rule still holds: the snapshot derives nothing of its own.
- **2026-10-06-snapshot-state-fidelity** — a password's (later also a file input's) `value` reads `MASKED_VALUE`. A part's lines must carry the same mask as the whole text, so the cut has to reuse the per-line form and never re-read values.
- **2026-10-06-compact-snapshot-serialization** — built `Snapshot::to_text` as the one pure text form, one line per node in pre-order, two spaces per depth, and `SNAPSHOT_TEXT_BUDGET` = 10,000 as "a ceiling a caller compares against, never enforced by `to_text`". It states "No `Display` impl, second entry point, parser, JSON form, truncation or paging". A part-cut is the first paging, so it touches this record directly. Its standing rule also applies: each new surface the text leaves by is asked again. The lean screens measured 755 · 1269 · 1259 · 2034 bytes.
- **2026-10-07-change-tracking-and-diff** — `Snapshot::diff` → `SnapshotDiff`, flat and by id, with no text form or budget. An acting verb's diff on a large screen has no bound of its own, which bears on the scope's open question of whether diffs or findings over the budget are bounded or stated as a limit.
- **2026-10-07-driver-session** (founder's rulings entry) — ratified that the snapshot text and diff leave only as the returned value. This precedent was later widened per carrier, so any new carriage of a part's text is weighed against it.
- **2026-10-07-driver-session** (registration entry) — escher-driver depends on no app; the caller boots it. Standing: the first request that carries screen content is a crossing question. The fixture for the walk therefore comes from the test caller, not the library.
- **2026-10-07-command-and-refusal-schema** — `VERBS` is the one table, and "a later entry adds its verbs to `VERBS`, never a second table". `validate` receives no `Session`. `Command`/`Call`/`ArgValue` print ids under `Debug`, so nothing fields them. Any part argument or verb lands as a row here with its `ArgKind` bounds.
- **2026-10-07-act-by-id** — `Session::run` is the one entry point and "nothing public runs a `Command` that did not come from `validate`". It defined `Outcome::Screen` for `snapshot`. The walk must be reached through `run`; a part answer is a new `Outcome` variant or a changed `Screen`.
- **2026-10-07-refusal-detection** — `scroll` is a precedent for a new instance verb ratified by the founder ("a verb joins `VERBS`, never a second table"). It also built the record of ids `SeenIds` (≤ `MAX_SEEN_IDS` 4096), which a part read must feed like `snapshot` does, with `stale`/`not-found` resolved against it.
- **2026-10-07-driver-command-spans** — the `command` span's eight fixed fields. `snapshot` records `verb` alone, and the screen text or its length is never fielded. A part's index, count or size stays off the span.
