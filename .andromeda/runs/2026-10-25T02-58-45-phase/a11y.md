- SC 2.1.1 may be claimed only as the plan scopes it. a11y-plan §5 Keyboard Navigation («Keyboard handling») says it is met by measurement on the stand's buttons through Enter and Space activation. a11y-plan §5 Keyboard Navigation («Test harness pattern») bounds the Tab-order proof (SC 2.4.3) to `stand_keyboard` walking each lean task's stated order forward and back, in both layout modes.
- SC 1.4.3 may be claimed only as a11y-plan §6 Visual Design Verification («Color contrast») scopes it: met on every measured non-exempt stand row, a row being exempt exactly where the snapshot's `enabled` reads `Some(false)`. The lint's `contrast` reading follows the same exempt rule, with no second formula or threshold.
- The statement must name the proof surface as the plan records it: the `a11y` leg runs fourteen files, and eight of them also run three `stand-repeat` rounds under the zero-failure budget. The leg has no required status check, so the statement must not call it a merge gate (per a11y-plan §9 CI Integration).
- The stated limits must carry the a11y gaps the plan records:

  - A `multiple` select and an `optgroup`'s options are not modelled, and no popup is painted (per a11y-plan §5 Keyboard Navigation, «Keyboard handling»).
  - `aria-activedescendant` is unread: the tree's focus is the CRUD list, never its option (per a11y-plan §5 Keyboard Navigation, «Per-surface keyboard shortcuts»).
  - The tree never states a native checkbox or radio as checked. The descriptions and the newly merged closed values reach only the adapter, so what an assistive technology reads of them is unmeasured (per a11y-plan §2 A11y Strategy, «Role derivation»).
- The snapshot-side limits a consumer reads must also be stated: no snapshot field says an element reacts to a click, which is owned by a residuals line, and `enabled` reads the presence of `disabled` (per a11y-plan §7 Screen Reader Support, «Accessibility tree output»). Whether README.md or any current escher document already states these limits is research's question.

## Patterns to follow
- State each measured fact with its witness, and each gap with its owner. The plan does this throughout, e.g. «proven headlessly on the stand … its windowed witness at the platform adapter owed — owned by a `.andromeda/residuals.md` line (target: next)» (per a11y-plan §2 A11y Strategy, «Accessibility tree lifecycle»).
- Name the witness by test file, as the leg's fourteen-file list does, rather than paraphrasing a proof (per a11y-plan §9 CI Integration).
- Don't write a second mapping. Roles, names and state readings are stated once (the shared module's tables, the snapshot model reading the tree) and never mapped again (per a11y-plan §7 Screen Reader Support, «Accessibility tree output»). The statement should point at the masters for these readings, not restate them.
