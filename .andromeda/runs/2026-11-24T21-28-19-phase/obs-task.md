Among them at least a label (a glyph run) overflowing its region; other checks over the recorded commands (a command outside the content box, a zero-area shape, text contrast) are candidates left to P3/P4.
How a canvas finding reaches the caller (new closed defect words in the lint answer, a field of the canvas answer, or in process only) is a crossing fork, written to evidence/founder-forks.md, and the phase stops there.
Proved by a planted fixture whose canvas label overflows and is named, and by the stand (Circle Drawer's canvas) reading clean, in both layout modes; re-reads the Circle Drawer canvas cross-OS shot digest watch.
