# obs extract

## Relevance
partial — the coverage floor and flakiness budget are test/CI gates with no instrumentation of their own; obs touches the chunk through CARRY 4 (the `command` span's `busy` field has no real line), CARRY 10 (the stderr-by-level readings not taken), CARRY 5 (the apt-install diagnostic), the coverage leg's artifact, and the silence rule every new or changed check must keep. obs-plan §10 SLO Invariants & Telemetry Budgets reads `NO RECORDED INTENT` and §11 Obs Anti-Patterns reads `NO RECORDED INTENT`, so neither is cited. The one keyed §3 contract, "Bootstrap phases", is Foundation-epoch bootstrap and does not apply here.

## Constraints
- CARRY 4: obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage requires the `command` span (target `escher_driver`, INFO, opened in `Session::run`) to record `busy` only when the outcome carries a busy class. The value must be the schema's word for that class, one of three closed words. The span must keep exactly its eight fields. The section records `busy` as "not driven" because no stand step reads not settled, so a unit test in `execute.rs` is its only cover. Whether a `stand_settle` fixture that ends at the settle bound can be reached through `Session::run`, and so print a real `busy=` line, is research's question.
- obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage lists what is never fielded on the `command` span or the `tool_call` span: `in_view`, `advanced_ms`, screen text or its length, a shot's image, byte size or dimensions, the session label, the record of ids, any argument, and any `Command`, `Call`, `ArgValue`, `Outcome`, `Refusal` or `Fault`. The `tool_call` span holds `tool` and `ending` only. A gate or a witness this chunk adds must not widen either span.
- obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing & Compliance (Scrubbing) requires escher's sink to keep its three outcomes. Engine targets print only the safe fields. `escher_*` targets print every field except the content-named set. Every other target is dropped whole at every level. Any new sink capture this chunk takes (a `busy` line, or a client's or windowed binary's stderr by level) is judged by that rule and must read 0 id, 0 name and 0 typed-sentinel needles.
- obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract (Logging stack) requires that stand checks and their shared modules install no subscriber, read no env var and print nothing. The named exceptions are `stand_act_spans`'s two re-run children, `stand_mcp_tools`'s in-process `Recorder`, and the re-executed children of `stand_id_persistence` and `stand_screenshot_repeat`, whose stdout goes into the parent's assertions only. Any check this chunk adds or extends, such as a repeat or flake witness, a `busy` witness or the hint-less `@font-face` check (CARRY 9), keeps that silence. A check that spawns a sink-installing binary reads its streams into assertions only. Where it asserts an accepted command's empty stderr, it removes `RUST_LOG` from the child.
- obs-plan §2 Telemetry Strategy (Feature wiring) states that a whole-workspace build (`--workspace`, `ci-leg.sh fast`, CI's test leg, and so the coverage leg's `cargo llvm-cov --workspace`) turns `blitz-dom/tracing` on, while a `-p blitz-tests` or `-p seven_guis` build leaves it off. Log-line counts and compiled code therefore differ by invocation. Any per-OS or per-build tally the chunk reads (CARRY 8) or floor it sets must name its build. The same section records the windowed `seven_guis_native`'s stderr by level under a workspace build as not measured (CARRY 10).
- obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing & Compliance (Values logged as-is) records the session host's by-level reading on one snapshot · click · type flow on CRUD only. It records "a client's own stderr by level", the windowed binary and any other flow as not measured. Closing CARRY 10's three readings means taking them under both builds, with the same needle counts that section uses.
- obs-plan §9 CI Integration (Telemetry artifact handling) fixes the coverage artifact. `target/coverage/lcov.info` is uploaded only on success as `coverage-report`, from `target/coverage/`, kept 7 days, and is the one fork-CI artifact outside `target/ci-logs/`. Per-leg logs go to `target/ci-logs/{leg}.log` and are uploaded only on failure. The same section bounds `.github/scripts/apt-install.sh`'s diagnostic (CARRY 5) to one stderr line per failed attempt: attempt number, phase and exit status, nothing of a user's.

## Patterns to follow
- An in-memory sink capture: a re-run ignored child installs `escher_telemetry::init_with_writer` at `RUST_LOG=info`, drives `Session::run` in process, and reads the capture into assertions only. A failing parent relays the child's own failure message, never a captured line. This is the shape a `busy` witness would take (`stand_act_spans`, per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract).
- A spawned-binary log check at `trace` under both builds (`-p seven_guis` and `--workspace`) that drains both streams from the spawn on and counts id, name, typed-sentinel and base64 needles. On failure it prints a kind and a count, never what the host wrote (`host_log` and `mcp_log`, per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract and §8 PII Scrubbing & Compliance).
- The silence probe pattern `println!|eprintln!|print!|dbg!|std::env|env::var|escher_telemetry|tracing_subscriber|UnixListener|TcpListener|bind\(`, run over every new or changed check file and shared module and expected to read 0 (per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract).
- Repeat runs read through the agent-run harness's JSON-line events: one `test {file, test, outcome}` per libtest result line, and `run.end` with counts. The raw `run.log` is never printed, and captured test output never reaches an event (per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage, the agent-run harness's log format).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Never add a field to the `command` or `tool_call` span to carry a gate reading, a repeat index, a call-supplied value or a shot's size. Both spans keep their closed field sets (per obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage).
- Never write a captured child's stderr or stdout text into an assertion message, a test name, a CI log line or committed evidence. Report counts and kinds only (per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract and §8 PII Scrubbing & Compliance).
- Never add an unconditional `println!`, an env read or a subscriber install to a stand check or its shared modules outside the named exceptions (per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract).

## Contract bindings
- obs ↔ tests §3 (the agent-run test contract): a flakiness budget measured through `scripts/agent-run.sh` reads obs-plan §6's harness event shape (`test` and `run.end`). Changing that shape is a binding change on both sides.
- obs ↔ tests §9 (the coverage leg and CI artifacts): obs-plan §9 records the `coverage-report` artifact's path, condition and retention, and the per-leg log's failure-only upload. A floor that turns the coverage leg red changes when that artifact exists. Whether to keep a red leg's lcov is a test-plan §9 and `.github/scripts/test_ci_workflows.py` matter that obs-plan §9 must then restate.
- obs ↔ security §Logging and §Input Validation: the scrub sets and the needle-0 readings in obs-plan §8 are the security plan's log-content guarantee. A new stderr reading (CARRY 10) or a `busy` line (CARRY 4) is evidence on both sides.
- obs ↔ tests (`stand_settle` fixtures): CARRY 4's real `busy` line depends on a fixture that ends at the settle bound being reachable by a driver step. That is a test-harness question with an obs reading.

## Acceptance criteria contributions
- If CARRY 4 is closed by a real line, a sink capture at `RUST_LOG=info` of a driver step that does not settle carries `span="command"` with `busy=` set to one of the three busy-class words and `settled=false`, no field beyond the eight, and 0 id, name and typed-text needles. If it is not closed, it stays recorded as unit-test-only (per obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage).
- Every check file and shared module this chunk adds or changes reads 0 on the silence probe, apart from the named exceptions (per obs-plan §3 Observability Harness Contract).
- Any stderr-by-level reading this chunk takes (a client, another flow, or the windowed binary under a workspace build) is recorded with its build and level and reads 0 id, 0 name and 0 typed-sentinel needles (per obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing & Compliance).
- After the coverage floor lands, `target/coverage/lcov.info` still carries line counts only, and its upload path, condition and retention either match obs-plan §9 or are restated there by the wrap. `apt-install.sh`'s diagnostic still writes only the attempt number, phase and exit status (per obs-plan §9 CI Integration).
