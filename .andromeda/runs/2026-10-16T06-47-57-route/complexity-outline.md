## Project signals (from arch + input)

- **Product type:** Rust library workspace for an HTML/CSS engine (an adopted Blitz fork), turned into an agent-first UI framework. Its headless driver ships as a library (`escher-driver`), a command line and an MCP server (arch §Project Intent, `Origin: adopted at 0f60502e`).
- **Surfaces in current scope:** the driver's two agent surfaces, CLI and MCP over stdio (one verb schema). There are no new surfaces, because 0.2.0 adds verbs and readings inside them. The windowed stand is exercised headless only.
- **Crates / modules:** 37 units in arch §Existing Scopes (packages, apps, examples, tests, wpt). escher's own are `escher-telemetry`, `escher-driver` and the `seven_guis` stand, with blitz-dom and dioxus-native-dom carrying escher changes.
- **Capabilities (Established Decisions count):** 29 items in arch §Established Decisions. All of them are as-built, so per the adopted-project rule they do not shape the epochs.
- **Standard Contracts:** 22 items in arch §Standard Contracts. The ones 0.2.0 touches are Driver session, Document core, Events, Scrolling, Test harness, Headless stand and Dioxus DOM bridge.
- **Cross-cutting concerns:** 10 items in arch §Cross-cutting Patterns. The relevant ones are invalidation and the changed set, event processing, and logging with the scrub sink.
- **Security tier (cross-input):** tier 0 (input.md `security_tier: 0`). Every new crossing or widening is ratified by the founder, and a widening, a new carrier or an engine change for every document is his fork.
- **Project signals from input.md:** agent-driven development style, every tier 0, a11y target SC 2.1.1 · 1.4.3 · 2.4.3. The version intent adds that escher leads Viola's UI as its first real consumer.
- **Existing Scopes:** 37 rows (arch §Existing Scopes). 0.1.0 is complete: 43 master records, all `complete`, 0 gated.

## Epoch outline (5 epochs)

### Epoch 1 — Foundation

- **Scope:** the codebase made ready to grow on the version-end audit's findings, before any new capability. This follows the founder's word that the first 0.2.0 chunk is the improvement.
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 2
- **Concerns:**
  - escher's over-ceiling handlers split along their range and activation arms, and `parse_float` split (v020-01; audit M1)
  - escher's inline test modules moved out of `document.rs`, and `mcp.rs` / `schema.rs` split along their seams (v020-01; audit M2, M3)
  - the six timeout-only mutants made to fail a test fast, with the score held at 0 survivors (v020-02; audit §Mutation tier)

### Epoch 2 — Engine debts

- **Scope:** the 0.1.0 engine deferrals as one entry right after the improvement (the founder's «Один пункт «долги движка» (Рекомендую)»). Each debt comes to the founder as its own fork. An upstream sync opens the epoch, because the debts sit in upstream-owned merge surface.
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 2
- **Concerns:**
  - upstream sync at the epoch boundary (intent §Principles, "Kept from 0.1.0")
  - the nine debts, each fixed, pinned or kept by the founder's fork (v020-03; nine residual lines absorbed)

### Epoch 3 — Determinism and traces

- **Scope:** one virtual clock under every headless run, then a driver session kept as a replayable CI check. The clock goes first because a replay holds only on a deterministic run.
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 4
- **Concerns:**
  - upstream sync at the epoch boundary
  - one virtual clock that only the driver moves, with the scrollbar fade on it (v020-06; residual absorbed)
  - a driver session recorded as a check through the CLI and MCP (v020-04)
  - the recorded check replayed as ordinary test input in CI, failing on a behaviour change (v020-04)

### Epoch 4 — Visual lint and real-app readiness

- **Scope:** the agent sees the defects it did not ask about, and reaches what a real frontend has: loads, `select` controls and screens over the budget. Each is proven on fixtures, ahead of Viola's planning.
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 5
- **Concerns:**
  - upstream sync at the epoch boundary
  - the visual-lint command: overlap, clipping, truncated text, off-screen, zero size, contrast, each finding by stable id with its measured reason (v020-05)
  - a load in flight waited out within a stated bound (v020-07; residual absorbed)
  - a `select` driven by id and read in the snapshot (v020-08; residual absorbed)
  - a screen over the snapshot budget read in parts (v020-09)

### Epoch 5 — Polish & ship

- **Scope:** every 0.2.0 capability proven headless, escher's capabilities stated where Viola's planning reads them, and the cold-agent run judged against the founder's bar.
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 4
- **Concerns:**
  - upstream sync at the epoch boundary
  - a requirement sweep: every 0.2.0 capability proven on the stand or a fixture (intent §Principles)
  - the capability statement for a consumer (v020-10)
  - the cold-agent run over the new capabilities against the founder's bar (v020-11; residual absorbed)

## Notes for Phase 1 synthesis

- The founder fixed the order: Foundation (codebase), then Engine debts, then the rest. The route sets the order of
  F1 · E1 · C1. C1 comes first by infrastructure-ahead: a replayable check and a repeatable shot both rest on one
  clock, and a bounded load wait needs one.
- Upstream sync opens each epoch after Foundation, as in 0.1.0. Foundation has none, because the founder's word puts
  the improvement first.
- Engine debts stays ONE chunk (the founder's «Один пункт»). Its forks are the founder's at that chunk's phase, never
  pre-decided here.
- The real-app proof on Viola's UI is outside this version (intent §6). The fixtures carry the proof here, and the
  capability statement (v020-10) is what Viola's planning reads.
- Phase 1 checks each plan's `Bootstrap phases` list for anything still recorded absent. Polish & ship holds only
  what the masters record absent.
