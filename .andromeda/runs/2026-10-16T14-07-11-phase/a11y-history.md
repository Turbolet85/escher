## Relevant amendment history

- **2026-10-13-stand-keyboard-harness** (Enter and Space activate a focused control, and a click focuses a button) — added the keyboard-activation arms that this chunk extracts from `handle_key_or_input_event` and `handle_key_up`, and the button-focus arm it extracts from `handle_click`. A pressed Enter, after the text-input and range arms, dispatches a Click on `button` · `summary` · `input` of type `submit`/`reset`/`button` · `a` with `href`. A released Space (KeyUp's first default action) does the same on `button` · `summary` · `input` of type `submit`/`reset`/`button`/`checkbox`/`radio`. In both cases there must be no `disabled` attribute, and the app's handlers run before `handle_click`. A pointer click on a button or a submit/reset/button input focuses it before the form-owner submit, while a `disabled` attribute still stops the walk and any other click clears focus. These are engine changes for every document and a boundary widening, ratified by the founder («Клавиши нажимают (Рекомендую)», «Клик даёт фокус (Рекомендую)», 2026-10-13, confirmed 2026-10-14). That is why the gates, the arm order and the handler-before-`handle_click` order cannot move under a refactor label: any change is a founder fork. It also moved the diff of §7: the Book names `flight-book` `[focused, bounds]`, so a lost focus mark would show there.
