@task_prose part=chunk
Lets an app declare virtual nodes inside a canvas widget, each with an author key (non-empty, no slash), a role from a closed set, a non-empty name and a region inside the canvas's content box.
A declaration breaking any rule is not taken: no node stands for it and nothing panics; a duplicate key in one canvas is refused.
Each taken node gets the reserved id form, the canvas's stable id, the virtual key separator, then the key, and the declared nodes are readable in process, validated and with their ids.
It adds no driver verb, cause, session error, exit status, carrier, log field or span; whether a declared name or key crosses to any carrier is the founder's fork, written with the declaration's form and the other forks into the founder-forks evidence, and the phase stops at P4 with no default.
Two CARRYs ride it to its wrap: the security summary's drag-and-drop and dropped-file-reader gaps, and the one-host clause of the no-screenshot-step decision; neither is written by hand.
