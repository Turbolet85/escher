## Patterns to follow
- Detail groups are fenced by `<!-- detail: values · {subject} -->` … `<!-- /detail -->` with a `Detail:` pointer under each body heading (design-system §Color Palette and every section after it); a row leaving a group leaves the group well-formed, and a group left empty is handled as the door's tooling requires (research reads which).
- A move of stand rows lands as an amendment, recorded through `.andromeda/design-system-amendments.md` beside the receiving masters' amendment files, not as a direct spec edit at phase (per the scope's own boundary on spec sources).
- Existing stand homes to extend: `.andromeda/layout-templates.md` already carries seven_guis Home/TaskShell, served screens and the flight booker's mode region; `.andromeda/architecture-detail/standard-contracts.md` carries the headless stand contract (`seven_guis::stand`, `boot_load`) — the natural landing for the DejaVu Sans boot item, while colour/shadow/spacing rows have no layout-templates precedent yet (research decides the section).

- Leaving a moved row in both design-system and its new home, or dropping the product rows that sit in the same groups (browser, WASM, engine defaults), per design-system §Color Palette › Semantic Colors and design-system §Typography.
