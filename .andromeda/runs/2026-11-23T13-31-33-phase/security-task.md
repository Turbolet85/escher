@task_prose part=chunk
Measures what a headless shot of a canvas, a custom widget painted from Rust, reads on each CI host (linux x86_64, macOS aarch64, windows x86_64) in both layout modes, taken as the PNG's bytes and as the decoded RGBA raster, so an encoder difference is told apart from a rasterizer difference.
The measurement reaches macOS and Windows only through the CI matrix, by a push to the build branch, and its cost in wall-clock and runner minutes is stated.
The readings go to the founder as one fork: canvas-shot byte equality across hosts required, amending the no-shot cross-OS rule, or held within a tolerance he sets; the fork is written to the founder-forks evidence and the chunk stops there.
After his word, a stand check holds a canvas shot on every CI OS to that word, equal to one committed table or within the tolerance, read where the matrix's whole-workspace test run and the linux test leg already run every harness test target.
It re-reads the no-shot rule's stated basis, that a shot's PNG bytes differ between linux x86_64 and macOS aarch64, at HEAD for a canvas shot.
@task_item n=1 part=needs
The canvas content of a headless shot: a custom widget's painted content crosses only as shot pixels, in the screenshot answer's PNG; the measurement and the stand check read those pixels and add no other way out for them.
@task_item n=2 part=needs
No widening of any ratified boundary: no new driver verb, argument, carrier, result field, refusal cause, session error, exit code, log field or span; a shot's pixels cross only as they already do, in band, and the boundary widening rule holds the chunk to that.
@task_item n=3 part=needs
The shot encoder: the PNG crate that encodes a screenshot is the one whose bytes are measured against the decoded raster; the decode for the comparison takes no new encoding or base64 crate.
@task_item n=4 part=needs
The custom widget seam the canvas fixture paints through: painted and pointed in its content box, handed the document's font context for its text, with the harness turning on the paint crate's custom-widget feature.
@task_item n=5 part=needs
The canvas fixture is a stand fixture booted alone, as the earlier canvas shot, placement and text stands boot theirs; a fixture state name it uses is checked against the closed fixture table.
@task_item n=6 part=needs
The CI matrix as the only macOS and Windows host: a probe pushed to the build branch runs every job of the matrix, the per-OS test jobs wait on the fast legs, and the workflow's concurrency cancels an open run of the same ref; the probe's cost is that run's wall-clock, its runners billing no minutes on the public repository.
@task_item n=7 part=needs
CI reading the measurement back: run logs and their annotations are read through the gh CLI under the workflow's read-only token, and nothing the probe adds reaches a secret or a publish path.
@task_item n=8 part=needs
The kept check keeps no pixels, and the committed stand-Counter checks stay free of a screenshot step unless the founder's word amends that rule too.
@task_item n=9 part=needs
The cross-OS no-shot rule this chunk's fork would amend: today no cross-OS byte check holds a shot; until the founder's word the stand check is written to neither keep nor compare shot bytes across hosts.
@task_item n=10 part=needs
Screen content stays out of logs: what the measurement prints of a difference is counts, sizes or digests of the raster and the PNG, never the pixels or the canvas's text, and no escher target gains a content-named field.
@task_item n=11 part=needs
Evidence written by the chunk, the founder-forks record and any measured reading, holds no credentials and no raw log naming a temp or home path; a readings table carries the host, the layout mode and the digest or count alone.
@task_item n=12 part=needs
The stand check after the founder's word: one committed table of expected digests or a stated tolerance, read on every CI OS, under locked builds, with its red reading shown before it is called green.
@task_item n=13 part=needs
The bundled fonts the canvas text paints with: the glyph coverage measured is that of the fonts the harness bundles, and a shot whose fonts are not ready is refused as fonts-unavailable rather than taken.
