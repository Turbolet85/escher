@extract_section name=constraints
- A probe check under tests/blitz-tests/ or examples/seven_guis/ installs no subscriber, reads no env var and prints nothing, and its readings go into assertions only. This matches the silence census every stand and engine check holds (per obs-plan §3 Logging stack (subscriber installation), the headless stand `seven_guis::stand` row, and per obs-plan §3 detail absence · logs).
- Nothing the probe reads (a component name, a handler or listener event name tied to a node, a signal's value) may enter a log line, span field or print. obs-plan §4 requires escher-driver's `command` span to keep its eight closed-word, bool and count fields and the `tool_call` span its two (per obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage). The probe adds no field to either.
- The probe adds no log site, span, field, log field or env read in packages/dioxus-native-dom/ (`dioxus_document.rs`, `events.rs`). obs-plan §8 records that dioxus-native-dom's debug `trace!` sites already carry text-node contents and attribute values unscrubbed when the `tracing` feature is on (per obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing & Compliance, Values logged as-is). A probe run must not turn that path into a reading or a print.
- Which build runs the probe decides whether engine events reach a sink: a workspace build turns `blitz-dom/tracing` on, while `-p blitz-tests` or `-p seven_guis` leaves it off (per obs-plan §2 Telemetry Strategy, Feature wiring). Measurements must not depend on engine log output.
- The MountedData `RefCell already borrowed` panic, when it is measured in a process that installed escher's sink, passes through escher-telemetry's chaining panic hook. That hook logs one ERROR at target `escher_telemetry::panic` with `panic.payload` redacted, and the chained std hook still prints the raw message to stderr (per obs-plan §7 Error Capture & Reporting, Panic hooks). Headlessly in process, the stand has no sink (per obs-plan §3 Logging stack (subscriber installation)). The probe's measurement takes the panic from the test outcome and copies no captured stderr into its reading.
- packages/escher-telemetry/ and packages/escher-driver/ stay unchanged. escher-driver keeps its 2 `info_span!` sites, and `host_log` and `mcp_log` keep reading 0 needles at `trace` under both builds (per obs-plan §3 detail absence · logs, the upstream take read at `e42ee1f25`).
