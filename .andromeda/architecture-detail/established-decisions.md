<!-- detail: values · dec-build-profiles -->

- **[Build profiles]** `dev` sets `debug = "line-tables-only"` — one debuginfo level for the dev host and CI, inherited by the `test` profile (Cargo.toml:208-209); it cut a blitz-tests binary's debuginfo share from 82 % to 55 % and the cold local baseline (build + blitz-tests + workspace tests) from 2239.59 s to 161.36 s, as measured at escher-0.1.0/chunks/2026-10-05-fork-ci-reached/evidence/baseline-rerun.md (dev host, 32 CPUs).
